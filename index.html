What makes Istanbul special?

The city combines iconic landmarks, lively streets, and waterfront views across the Bosphorus—ideal for culture-focused, budget-friendly travel.

Simple travel planning tip

Plan by zones: Historic Peninsula (landmarks), Galata & Beyoğlu (streets/tower), and Bosphorus (ferries/views). This reduces travel time and improves the visitor experience.

Technical quality

Pages share one CSS file, use semantic HTML, include alt text on images, and provide a keyboard “skip to content” link for accessibility.

Quick guide

Short, scannable cards for usability.

Best time to visit

Spring and autumn are usually comfortable for walking, sightseeing, and ferry rides.

Getting around

Use metro/tram for speed and ferries for scenic transport. A travel card saves time.

Must-do experiences

Historic sites, Grand Bazaar browsing, Istiklal Street, and a sunset ferry on the Bosphorus.
