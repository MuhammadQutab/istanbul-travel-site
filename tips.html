Travel tips for Istanbul

Practical guidance written for quick reading.

Planning and budget

  • Plan 2–3 major activities per day to avoid rushing.
  • Use metro/tram where possible to reduce travel time and cost.
  • Carry small cash for markets and small local purchases.

Comfort and navigation

  • Save locations offline (maps) before leaving accommodation.
  • Group attractions by area (Historic Peninsula / Galata / Bosphorus).
  • Bring a portable charger for navigation and tickets.

Respect, safety, and etiquette

  • Dress respectfully in religious sites and stay quiet during prayers.
  • In crowded places (bazaar, transport), keep valuables secure.
  • Wear comfortable shoes—some streets are hilly or uneven.
